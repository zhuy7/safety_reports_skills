## 中文摘要
2025年7月27日，东莞大朗镇一在建民房电梯安装过程中，无证施工人员龚万林未佩戴安全带，从17米高无防护平台坠落身亡。事故主因是安全管理缺失、违规搭建脚手架、未设生命线及持证上岗制度落实不到位。劳务承包人徐艳兵及速达公司等单位存在管理缺陷，项目经理未履职，出借资质致监管缺位。

## English Summary
On July 27, 2025, during elevator installation at a construction site in Dalang, Dongguan, unlicensed worker Gong Wanlin fell 17 meters from an unprotected platform without wearing a safety belt, resulting in fatal injuries. The primary causes included safety management failure, improper scaffold construction, lack of lifelines, and failure to enforce certification requirements. Labor contractor Xu Yanbing, Suda Company, and others exhibited management deficiencies, with project managers failing to supervise and qualification lending leading to regulatory gaps.
